# Presentation validation

- Render all slides at the intended aspect ratio.
- Inspect safe margins, overlaps, clipping, contrast, font substitution, image resolution, and chart readability.
- Verify slide order, titles, notes, citations, links, alt text, and required brand elements.
- Compare exports against the editable source and record any unsupported animation or media.
- Keep source deck, rendered preview, export, and validation Artifact IDs distinct.
